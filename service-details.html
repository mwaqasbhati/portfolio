How I work

Take over and move forward — or build correctly from the start

If your product already has code, I understand it before changing it. If it is half-built or AI-generated, I turn it into something maintainable and ready for production. If it is just an idea, I help shape the technical direction and ship it.

What I can help with

  • Codebases built by someone else that need a proper technical review
  • AI/vibe-coded MVPs that need cleanup, structure and maintainability
  • Half-built products that need to be completed and shipped
  • New products from idea to production
  • Features, integrations and improvements without breaking existing functionality
  • Performance, architecture, bugs and technical debt

Coverage

Web, mobile, backend, APIs, databases, payments and third-party integrations. I work as a full-stack product engineer across the lifecycle, not as a handoff-only developer.
